Application

How to apply your set

No salon needed — a clean, confident press-on application in under ten minutes.

01

Prep

Push back cuticles, lightly buff the nail surface, then wipe with the prep pad to remove oils.

02

Match sizes

Lay your press-ons out and match each one to the right finger using your sizing guide.

03

Apply

Apply a thin layer of glue, or place an adhesive tab, on your natural nail.

04

Press & hold

Press on starting at the cuticle, then down to the tip. Hold firmly for 15–20 seconds.

05

Finish

Gently file any overhang at the sides for a clean, salon-smooth finish.

Good to know

FAQs & care guide

How do I find my correct size?

Send us a clear photo of your nails on WhatsApp and we'll match you to the right fit before you pay — see the sizing section above.

How long do press-ons last?

Each set is reusable for 3–4 wears with proper care and storage.

How do I remove them without damaging my nails?

Soak your hands in warm soapy water for a few minutes, then gently wiggle each nail from the sides — no acetone soak required.

Can I reuse a set?

Yes — keep the nails clean of old glue and store them in their original packaging between wears.

How long does application take?

A few minutes per hand once your sizing is confirmed.

What if a nail lifts or pops off?

Reapply with a small dab of glue. If it keeps happening, message us on WhatsApp and we'll help sort the fit.
